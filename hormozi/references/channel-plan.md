# Nikolytics | Playbook 4: The Channel Ramp Plan (Final)

Produced 2026-07-03 via Playbook 4. Companion to flagship-offer.md and money-model.md (both approved and fixed; nothing here re-decides them). Fixed elements honored throughout: $250,000 Zero-Surprise Platform Sprint, $25,000 Blueprint (100% credit toward a build within 60 days, cap 2 concurrent), 3 concurrent build slots with published fill level, cohort kickoffs first and third Monday, Live-in-30 conditional guarantee.

## 1. Strategy at a Glance

**The fork, verified.** Nikolytics is cash-poor until ~$140k collects in late July and time-rich by commitment (4+ founder hours/day on acquisition). The Core Four sequence is therefore mandatory, not optional: **warm outreach first at Rule-of-100 volume, content daily alongside from week 1, cold outreach optional later, paid ads last and gated to Q1 2027 at the earliest.** An agency is engaged only with money to spare, on a learn-then-leave arrangement (Section 5.7).

**The ladder every channel feeds.** Magnet (free or cheap diagnostic) → **$25,000 Blueprint** (100% credit within 60 days, max 2 concurrent) → **$250,000 Sprint** (3 slots, fill level published, kickoffs first and third Monday). Nothing free ever duplicates the Blueprint (Section 2 scope wall).

**Honest conversion math (verified correction).** The book's ~1 customer per 100 warm reach-outs is low-ticket math (~$400 tickets) and does NOT transfer raw to a $250,000 close. What transfers: ~20% reply rates and the funnel shape. Expect 2-3+ conversations per high-ticket close (leads.md §6, §16) and multiple weeks of BAMFAM'd meetings per build; at this ticket, plan for more.

**Daily quotas.** 100 personalized warm reach-outs/day for 100 straight days (starting Wednesday 2026-07-08), plus 100 minutes/day of content with minimum 1 published post/day. **Graduation trigger to Open To Goal:** switch when EITHER (a) the 100 days complete, or (b) pipeline covers capacity, defined numerically: both Blueprint slots filled or reserved, the next two cohort kickoffs each holding a signed or verbally committed build, and 6+ qualified founder conversations live. Then: work until **2 new qualified funded-founder conversations are booked per day**, done by lunch or at hour 18, with a permanent floor of 20 reach-outs/day plus a monthly 9-word email to one lapsed segment.

**The gates.** Paid ads: Gate A (collected platform cash, $60k in a separate ads account, funded only by collected build/Blueprint payments), Gate B (end-to-end tracking verified), Gate C (90+ days of proven organic content). Referrals: the 25% Level-4 gate before ads re-scale beyond Tranche 3 of the Section 6 staged unlock (the permanent 1-10% test allocation always on). Every channel: LTGP:CAC ≥ 3:1 floor with real numbers (Section 7).

## 2. The Lead Magnet Suite

Every magnet follows the 7-step creation process (leads.md §2) and exists to reveal the spec gap only the Blueprint fixes. Bar pretzels: we solve hunger, we create thirst, the Blueprint sells the drink.

### Magnet 1 | The Launch-Risk Teardown (one diagnostic, two doors, live week 1)

Merge resolution: the warm-outreach "Launch-Risk Teardown" and the lead-magnet "Platform Rewrite-Risk Audit" were the same diagnostic wearing two names. They are now **one asset**: a 12-point rewrite-risk review of the prospect's existing stack, architecture, or vendor quotes (the same failure modes compiled in the Cheap Build Teardown from public post-mortems), delivered as a 90-minute session plus a scored 2-3 page written risk memo and a 5-minute recorded walkthrough forwardable to a cofounder or board. It is also the money model's rescue-rung briefing: one artifact, two doors.

- **Type:** Reveal Their Problem (the problem worsens as the launch window closes). **Vehicle:** services.
- **Capacity, unified:** **5 per week total** (4 weekday slots Tue-Fri plus 1 Saturday-morning overflow), delivered by Nikola. Per Teardown, honestly costed: ~3 hours of employee time (research prep before, memo first-draft from the session recording and scoring rubric after, ~$900 loaded), plus 90 founder minutes of session and ~30 founder minutes to finalize the scored memo and record the 5-minute walkthrough. The cap is the scarcity line in every CTA.
- **First five free (cardinal rule respected):** advisory and diagnostic ONLY. No co-signed spec, no prototype, no Acceptance Checklist, no architecture document, no credit. Explicit terms, said out loud before booking: "It's free, and free has four conditions. One, you use it: you show up and your team acts on the memo or tells me why not. Two, blunt feedback on the session. Three, if it's excellent, a killer review I can publish with your name, only if deserved. Four, the last five minutes go to your contacts: two or three funded founders, ideally as 3-way intros, not names." If someone refuses free: "What would I have to do to make it worth it for you?"
- **Free-then-ladder pricing:** trigger to charge = first free recipients start referring. List price $5,000; ladder: 5 free → 5 at $1,000 (80% off) → 5 at $2,000 → 5 at $3,000 → then +~20% every 5 sales. Ceiling: past ~$7,500 stop raising and route buyers straight to the Blueprint; the diagnostic always reads one tier below it.
- **Names to test:** (1) "The Launch-Risk Teardown: find out in 90 minutes whether your platform plan survives your next raise" (2) "The Board-Proof Stack Review for Funded Founders" (3) "Will It Survive Real Users? The 12-Point Platform Teardown."
- **Quality bar:** the memo tells the whole truth, including "your stack is fine, do not rebuild yet." The 99% who never buy still carry the reputation.
- **CTA, founder voice:** "Three of these twelve risks live in undefined scope, and no audit fixes undefined scope, only a co-signed spec does. That is the $25,000 Blueprint: spec, clickable prototype, locked Acceptance Checklist, 100% credited toward a build within 60 days. I run at most two Blueprints at a time and builds cap at three slots, currently [X] of 3 filled. Next kickoff is Monday the [date]; sign by Thursday and you are in it."

### Magnet 2 | The Real-Cost Build Estimator (software, ships weeks 4-6)

The felt pain: "my three vendor quotes span $60k to $400k and I cannot tell why." A web calculator (features, integrations, load, compliance in; honest cost-and-timeline **range** out, plus hidden line items cheap quotes omit). Email required for the full PDF, making every use an engaged lead. Every wide-range driver in the output is a spec unknown; the last page is the Blueprint CTA. Our own $250k/30-day band appears like any other option, line items filled in. Build cost ~3 internal dev-days; **scheduling resolution:** the engineers are mid-delivery, so the build is slotted into the first delivery gap (target weeks 4-6, second claim behind the first Blueprint pair-week, Section 8 week 4), never week 1. Names to test: (1) "What Should Your Platform Actually Cost? The Funded Founder's Build Estimator" (2) "The Quote-Gap Calculator: why your three quotes disagree by $300k" (3) "The Real-Cost Platform Estimator." This is the magnet that scales into content CTAs and Q1 2027 ads at ~$0 marginal delivery.

### Magnet 3 | The Paragons Build Diary (ungated now, gated Q4 2026)

Zero episodes exist today, so gating it now would fail the quality bar and constraint 9. It starts as ungated daily content (Section 4), one Content Unit per build day, every episode's ask pointing at Magnet 1 or 2. **Graduation gate:** Paragons build shipped plus 10+ episodes (target Q4 2026), then compile into a gated One-Step-of-a-Multi-Step-Process magnet. Names then: "The Glass Box Diaries" / "30 Days, Every Artifact" / "Watch Us Ship."

### Name-test procedure (headline is 80 cents of the dollar)

Test headline, then image, then subheadline. At zero spend: split the daily 100 reach-outs 50/50 between two name variants for the week's featured magnet; 3-day follow-up cadence held constant; read reply and take rates after ~300 sends per variant (~6 outreach days); declare a control, then challenge with variant 3. Parallel cheap read: a LinkedIn "reply 1 or 2" poll. These are directional controls, re-tested under ad traffic in Q1 2027 before paid scaling. Mega-winner signal: people ask when they can get it.

### Scope wall (constraints 2 and 3)

The Teardown diagnoses what exists; the estimator prices what is undefined; the diary shows how we work. None produces a spec, prototype, Acceptance Checklist, new-build architecture, or credit. Any prospect asking a magnet to do those hears, verbatim: "That is the Blueprint, it costs $25,000, and every dollar credits toward your build within 60 days."

## 3. The Warm Outreach System (primary channel)

### List consolidation (days 1-2, ~4 hours, employee-assisted)

One spreadsheet, one row per human: phone exports, Google Contacts from every account, LinkedIn connections export (likely the largest block), X followers/following, WhatsApp chats walked manually, and every contact from the Shane/American Paragons, Sport Endorse, and The Players Impact orbits. Never anything INTIIMI-related. Fields: name, best channel, handle, how I know them, segment, last touch, status, **source**. Segments: **A** funded-startup founders/operators; **B** people who know founders (VCs, angels, accelerator staff, startup lawyers); **C** everyone else. Benchmark 1,000+; if the list lands under 1,000, start anyway at full volume, log the true number day 2, and note the shortfall is what content and later cold outreach exist to fix. Exhaust the largest platform first.

### Quota, cadence, and honest time budget

**100 personalized reach-outs/day, 100 straight days**, up to 3 touches per person over 3 days or until reply. Evening prior: an employee batches 25-30 researched names with one personal fact each (~30 min). Morning: Nikola sends 100 openers in ~2 hours, then ~90 minutes of replies, A-C-A threads, and booked calls (overflow replies top the next morning's queue). Daily scorecard rule: you either sent 100 or you did not.

### Scripts

- **Openers (no pitch):** one personal line, one question. Text: "Saw you closed the seed with [fund], congrats. How's the build side going, hiring or outsourcing the platform?" LinkedIn: "Your post on [topic] was the most honest take I've read in months. Since [company] is post-raise, who's building the product day to day?" Re-warm 9-word email: "Are you still looking to ship your platform this year?" No links. Replies get top priority next morning.
- **A-C-A on replies:** Acknowledge ("so you're six weeks and five figures in and there's no clickable product"), Compliment ("pushing them instead of hoping says you take the launch date seriously"), Ask ("what did they commit to in writing, a live date or a process?"). Three to four exchanges, then the offer.
- **Who-do-you-know (Value Equation script), used with B and C, and with A after A-C-A:** "Do you know any funded founders, seed to Series B, whose platform build is stuck in discovery or duct-taped in no-code, who need the real thing live in the next couple of months? I'm taking on five free Launch-Risk Teardowns because five is genuinely all I can run alongside our builds. 90 minutes plus a written risk memo: I show exactly where their plan dies and what a defensible 30-day path looks like. If their plan is fine I'll say so in writing. Does anyone come to mind?" Ask who they know, never for them to buy; many volunteer themselves. One-liner for bios: "I help funded founders get their platform live in production in 30 days without becoming their dev team's project manager, guaranteed live or we work free until it is."

### Tracking and planning hypothesis

Daily log fields: date, name, segment, channel, touch #, replied, A-C-A live, call booked, Teardown offered/accepted, referral names captured, Blueprint conversation opened, Blueprint signed, build stage, cohort targeted. Weekly rollup against the 20% reply benchmark. Planning hypothesis, not a promise: first 30 days at full volume on a ~1,000 list → ~2,000-3,000 touches → ~200 replies → 25-40 real conversations → 8-12 Teardowns → 3-5 serious Blueprint conversations → 1-2 Blueprints signed → first build inside 60-90 days via money-model Scenario C. If Teardown takers have the problem and the money but nothing converts, that is a sales problem, not a channel problem.

### Graduation and throttle

Trigger defined in Section 1. When pipeline exceeds 3 build slots + 2 Blueprint slots, warm outreach throttles to the 20/day floor and surplus demand feeds the waitlist with its 72-hour claim window, never a discount.

## 4. The Content Engine (channel #2, daily from week 1)

78% of clients consume 2+ long-form pieces before booking; content silently nurtures every conversation the other channels generate.

**Platform:** LinkedIn only (passes all four selection criteria); depth-then-width. **X is named now for later**, added only when LinkedIn sustains 2-3 posts/day through the full 100-day run with reach still growing; then repurpose the greatest-hits list, never write net-new.

**Quota:** 100 minutes/day creating, minimum 1 published post/day, 100 days, manually posted by a human. Fixed slot 6:00-7:40 a.m. (60 write, 20 record/clip, 20 post and reply). Raw material is free: the Glass Box Build already produces daily demo videos, staging deploys, and decision logs, so content is mostly narration of work already done. The contract designer gets a capped 3 hours/week for carousel polish; the two engineers are never touched for content. Cadence ramp: 1/day days 1-30, 2/day days 31-60, 2-3/day days 61-100.

**Every post is a Content Unit** (Hook: five topic sources + 2 of 7 news elements + native format; Retain: lists, steps, stories; Reward: value per second, the audience decides). Weekly skeleton: Mon build log (Recent Past), Tue artifact (Manufactured: extraction agenda, Acceptance Checklist, Swap Token, migration dry-run format), Wed decision deep-dive ("How I chose Postgres for Shane's platform, and the 3 questions I asked first"), Thu scar tissue (Far Past, no client names, no invented numbers), Fri trending, Sat captured idea doubled down, Sun ASK. INTIIMI is never referenced. Zero performance metrics until real ones exist.

**Shane co-content barter, in writing before the first post.** Shane provides: three recorded milestone debriefs (kickoff, day-14 demo, go-live); written permission to publish decision-log excerpts, staging screenshots, and demo clips; 2+ Paragons posts/month sharing the series; one joint LinkedIn Live at go-live; a testimonial video ONLY if deserved; 48-hour review window, no approval means no publish. Nikola provides: edited clips back within 5 days; up to 4 designer-produced assets/month; full launch-day amplification. Disclosure line in every co-post: "I hold a small equity stake in Shane's umbrella company." (Referral asks to Shane belong to Section 5, not this barter.)

**Give:Ask at 4:1, intermittent.** Default ask = the current control lead magnet; roughly 1 in 3 asks may go to the Blueprint with capacity honesty: "Hard cap of 2 concurrent Blueprints, kickoffs first and third Monday, [X of 2] Blueprint seats and [X of 3] build slots open. DM me 'BLUEPRINT'." Then straight back to giving.

**Greatest-hits master list** from post 1 (URL, format, topic source, problem solved in avatar words, reach, saves): outreach sends the matching piece before and after calls; the top ~10% by reach and saves are flagged "ad-candidate" monthly with raw footage archived, so Q1 2027 ad creative is proven organic winners (Gate C).

**Metrics, monthly:** total followers and reach per platform, growth rate absolute and %. Content volume is explicitly not scored; cadence held constant inside each measurement month. Weekly Monday ritual: one hook test per platform, log everything, 4 failed attempts to beat the control → next constraint.

## 5. Referrals and Lead Getters

Adoption order: referrals always on → employees when Nikola is the constraint → agency only with money to spare → affiliates last.

**5.1 Monday tracking spec.** Before any outreach: mandatory `source` field on every lead: warm | content | cold | paid | referral:[name]. The permanent 1-10% test allocation runs regardless.

**5.2 This week's three asks, all 3-way introductions** (never a name and number; each ask targets two named intros, ceiling six by Friday). Shane, on the content call: "I run three build slots and want the next cohort filled by referral, not ads. Who are the two founders whose product is stuck between a cracking no-code stack and an agency quoting six months? One three-way text or email per founder with one line from you. If one becomes a build, I send you $10,000, cash or credited, your pick." Sport Endorse and The Players Impact: warm re-open first (A-C-A), then the same skeleton with the $10,000 fee.

**5.3 The seven ways to ask, mapped.** Referral CAC is near zero against ~$215k build LTGP, so compensation anchors to the paid-CAC governor: **$10,000 per closed build referral, $2,500 per Blueprint-only close** (~21:1 and ~5:1 LTGP:CAC, far above the 3:1 floor). #1 one-sided benefit: primary now. #3 ask right at purchase: standing rule at every signing ("who else in your investor's portfolio is fighting a platform build?"), two names to 3-way intros on the spot. #4 negotiation chip: price and the 54% deposit never move; "sign as written plus three 3-way intros, and I attach the Launch Concierge Command ($22,000, never sold separately) even past the Thursday lock." **5.4 Goodwill plays.** The 48-hour window after any signing (accounts live, kickoff calendar landed, first deliverable dated); scripted asks at the two big wins (day-14 demo, go-live); tell them what to buy next (Block at ~day 25, Engine at handoff); internal milestones padded +50% so clients experience early, never late (the 30-day promise itself untouchable).

**5.5 Shane's affiliate upgrade, trigger conditions only.** All three must hold: Paragons build live with case-study content published; ≥2 Shane referrals reaching a Blueprint Debrief; referral tracking running. Then the six steps, with payout tiers on the $48k max allowable CAC: $12,000 / $24,000 / $48,000 at signup / activation / sustained performance; launch via Whisper-Tease-Shout timed to the case-study drop; integrate at mode 1 ($5,000 Blueprint certificates); paid forever, never capped.

**5.6 Employee lead-getter trigger.** 60 consecutive working days at 100 reach-outs/day with build slots still under 3-of-3, OR delivery forcing acquisition under 4 hours/day for two straight weeks. First hire: content/outreach assistant ~$6,000/month, recruited via the Internal Core Four, trained via 3Ds until a stranger's checklist run matches Nikola's reply rates. Economics: $6,000/mo ÷ 60 engaged leads = $100/lead; solvency floor: total acquisition cost ≤ 1/3 of $215k LTGP ≈ $71k/client; one sourced build/year covers the salary at 3:1.

**5.7 Agency: learn-then-leave, three triggers.** (1) cumulative collected platform cash ≥ $500,000; (2) the Q1 2027 ads gates met; (3) $60k-$90k earmarked for 6 months from collected cash inside the $200k budget, without touching delivery payroll. Script: "Work with me for six months so I can learn how you do it. I'll pay extra for the why. Then I train my team and we drop to consulting." Select against the full 10-point checklist; run the internal person in parallel from day one; when internal beats agency consistently, drop to consulting, then cut loose.

## 6. The Paid Ads Runway (fully gated, Q1 2027 earliest)

Funded exclusively from collected platform payments. Never personal cash, never uncollected invoices.

**Gate A | Cash:** separate ads account holds $60,000 ($50,000 = one full test + $10,000 expected-loss pot), funded only by collected payments, AND the money model's Q3 gate (two builds closed and collected) hit. **Gate B | Tracking:** CRM with mandatory Lead Source and First-Touch fields (three existing clients backfilled); UTMs carried ad → form → CRM; booking tool passing hidden UTM fields; deal stages with 30-day collected cash per deal; LinkedIn Insight Tag plus offline-conversion upload. Verification: one seeded lead travels ad-click to closed-won with source intact. No pass, no spend. **Gate C | Content bank:** 90+ consecutive days of daily posts, a greatest-hits list labeled by problem solved, and 3+ pieces that each produced 5+ engaged leads; those 3 are the first creatives. We never write ads from scratch.

**Platform:** LinkedIn, single platform (all four criteria pass; Gate C doubles as native-format training). **Targeting:** the consolidated list (Section 3) seeds Matched Audiences once it clears ~300 members; until then layered factors: US, founder/CEO/CTO, headcount 11-200, software, plus organic-engager retargeting.

**Three Phases with real numbers.** Phase 1 Track Money = Gate B. Phase 2 Lose Money: test budget = 2x the 30-day collected cash of a new customer per ad; kill at 1x with zero engaged leads. Ads point at the magnet and the $25,000 Blueprint front end, never the $250k Sprint. The math that forces this: Blueprint funnel test ceiling **$50,000** per lead-producing ad, kill line **$25,000**; flagship-direct would need a $400,000-$500,000 ceiling with a kill line exceeding the entire annual budget, so it is mathematically untestable; the Blueprint funnel gives 4-8 honest reads per year instead of zero. Phase 3 Print Money: capacity caps demand at ~2 paid-sourced Blueprints/month, so budget = 2 x $20,000 x 1.2 = **$48,000/month maximum**, further capped at 20% of trailing-90-day collected cash.

**Economics.** Blended LTGP ≈ $280,000, so the 3:1 floor sits at a $93,000 build CAC and is not binding. The binding constraint is the derived 30-day governor: **paid build CAC ≤ ~$48,000 naked, ≤ ~$68,000 with the Traction Block**; at ≥60% Blueprint-to-build conversion, **Blueprint CAC ≤ ~$28,800 hard, $15,000-$20,000 working target**. Breach two weeks running → pause and run the forks (CAC ≤ 3x industry average → model problem; problem+money but no buy → sales problem). Permanent expected-loss allocation: 5% ≈ $10,000/year on zero-expectation tests, forever, never counted against any ad's CAC.

**Ad anatomy (Callout → Value → CTA), three What-Who-When angles banked:** (1) "Funded founders: is your next board meeting a launch or another Gantt chart?" → live in 30 days on infrastructure you own, 3 slots, published fill → Blueprint CTA with cohort date. (2) "Did your dev shop quote six months of discovery?" → the Cheap Build Teardown pattern → free magnet, then Blueprint. (3) "If you're mid-raise and your agency made you their project manager..." → under an hour a day → Blueprint, cohort date, fill level. **Click-to-close:** every ad lands on the Playbook 5 page carrying the same promise, look, and language; fill-level counter and cohort countdown match.

**The $200k staged unlock.** Tranche 1 $50,000 (months 1-3): two ads, kill each at $25,000 with zero engaged leads; unlock = all three gates green. Tranche 2 $50,000 (months 3-6): unlock = ≥1 lead-producing ad AND ≥1 ad-sourced Blueprint signed. Tranche 3 $90,000 (months 6-12): unlock = measured build CAC clearing the $48k/$68k governor, LTGP:CAC ≥ 3:1 end-to-end, and 30-day collected cash from ad-sourced clients ≥ trailing spend. Expected-loss pot $10,000 always on. Every tranche replenishes only from collected payments. **Re-scaling beyond Tranche 3 additionally requires the 25% Level-4 referral gate.** Scale path: MORE on the winner until the governor breaks → BETTER, one test/week at the biggest drop-off, Monday 60-minute ritual, 4 fails → next constraint → NEW (placements, then Meta with the same creative), last.
