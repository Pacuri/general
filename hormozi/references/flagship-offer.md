## 3. The Stack | Total Value: $465,000

The full 65-problem list (before / during / after, bucketed by the four inverted value drivers) reduces to nine clusters; every listed problem maps into exactly one stack item or bonus. One unaddressed perceived problem kills a $250k sale, so nothing is left unmapped.

**1. The Boardroom Business Case | $15,000**
Problem: "I might be spending a quarter million on an annoyance, and my board will ask why we outsourced our core product."
Solution: how to know, before signing, exactly what this build returns and how to defend it to a board.
Vehicles: ROI model tied to YOUR raise metrics (ARR, activation, pilot revenue); loaded-cost comparison (two senior hires at $400k/yr plus 6 months recruiting vs this); the Cheap Build Teardown, the documented reasons $60-80k offshore builds get rewritten, in board language; a board-deck-ready one-pager. If the verdict is "don't build," we tell you, and you keep the analysis.

**2. The Head-to-Blueprint Sprint | $35,000**
Problem: "The product lives in my head; the flow I forget becomes the feature you skip."
Solution: how to turn a founder's head and a messy Notion doc into a co-signed spec and clickable prototype before a line of code.
Vehicles: 2-3 structured extraction sessions run by the people who will actually build it; written spec plus clickable prototype of every core flow, signed before kickoff; architecture document your technical advisor can audit, with stack choices justified in writing; the co-signed Acceptance Checklist that later powers both the guarantee and the final payment.
Also sold standalone: **The Blueprint, $25,000**, with 100% of it credited toward the build if you proceed within 60 days, and Blueprint purchasers hold waitlist priority. This is the defined entry offer and the natural downsell.

**3. The Locked-Number Charter | $25,000**
Problem: "Every fixed quote balloons; and if we freeze scope, we freeze my worst day-zero ideas."
Solution: how to lock the price without freezing your thinking.
Vehicles: contractual rule that any ambiguity inside the signed Blueprint resolves in the client's favor at no charge; two Swap Tokens, each lets you replace an unbuilt feature with a comparable one mid-build at zero cost, so week-two customer discovery improves the product instead of torching the budget; change orders exist only for net-new scope you explicitly choose.

**4. The Glass Box Build | $300,000**
Problem: "I wired six figures to people I cannot verify, building something I cannot see, on a timeline nobody else believes."
Solution: the 30-day build itself, made impossible to fake.
Vehicles: three senior engineers named in the contract with a no-substitution clause (any change requires your written approval); live staging URL from day 3, deployed daily, click the real system whenever you want, no smoke-and-mirrors demo survives that; daily 3-minute end-of-day demo video; US business-hours overlap with blocker questions answered within 4 working hours; the **Integration War Room**: Stripe/Twilio/HubSpot sandboxes opened day one and the scariest third-party APIs attacked in week 1, when there is still slack; milestone dashboard your board can see. Why 30 days is real and not corner-cutting: prebuilt, hardened infrastructure modules (auth, billing, admin, deploy pipelines) that we reuse and you own, so senior time goes into YOUR product, not boilerplate. Post-deposit leverage stays with you: the work is visible daily, sits in your accounts (item 6), and on the split plan the final 20% is not due until you are live.
Value anchor: two senior hires cost $400k/yr loaded plus 6 months of recruiting before a line of code, and typical agencies quote 6-12 months for this scope.

**5. The One-Hour-a-Day Founder Protocol | $20,000**
Problem: "I am mid-fundraise. I cannot moonlight as your PM and QA department."
Solution: how to feed a 30-day build in under an hour a day.
Vehicles: asset concierge (we chase brand files, draft copy for approval, set up third-party accounts with you on a screen-share instead of sending homework); every design review arrives as A/B/C options, founders who know what they hate choose in minutes; three fixed decision gates booked around your partner meetings at signing; professional QA by us against the Acceptance Checklist, your final review is a guided 60-minute walkthrough, not an 11pm bug hunt.

**6. The Day-One Keys Protocol | $18,000**
Problem: "Do I actually own this, or am I captive? And what if this boutique shop folds?"
Solution: how to hold every key from before the first commit.
Vehicles: repo, cloud accounts, domains, CI, and API keys created under YOUR logins on day one, we work inside your accounts, never the reverse; full IP assignment; US-enforceable contract; documentation written for a stranger, so any competent team on earth can take over, which makes us permanently replaceable and therefore permanently accountable; **Migration Without Casualties**: dry-run migration on a copy, a parallel-run period where old and new systems run side by side, and a signed migration report with record counts and checksums before the real cutover.

**7. The Open-Ledger Quality Pack | $30,000**
Problem: "30 days must mean cut corners, and I cannot read code to check."
Solution: independent eyes and written evidence, so you never take our word.
Vehicles: an independent third-party code audit at handoff, performed by a firm you approve from a shortlist, with the report delivered to you directly; automated test suite and CI you watch run; security questionnaire answer pack for your enterprise pilot's procurement team; SOC 2-ready architecture checklist so compliance is later a configuration exercise, not a rebuild. (Internal: audit is a fixed per-build cost, budgeted inside the $250k margin.)

**8. Launch Command | $22,000**
Problem: "Code complete is not launched. Who is watching MY product at 2am on day one?"
Solution: we launch it, prove it scales, and hand you the instruments.
Vehicles: production deployment; monitoring and alerting built in your accounts, pointed at your phone; load test to 10x projected traffic with a written report; cloud cost-at-scale projection at 1x, 5x, 10x users; launch-week on-call by the team that wrote the code, and the kickoff calendar is spaced so a team's launch week closes before its next build begins, so this coverage lives inside that team's own slot, never a phantom one.

**Stack total: $465,000 itemized.** It solves all 65 problems, it is one of a kind, and there is nothing to compare it to: offshore shops have no charter, no audit, no keys protocol; agencies have no 30 days, no daily glass box, no locked number.

## 5. The Guarantee

Conditional, service-based, stated boldly with the reason why: "We do not offer money-back, because we front a named senior team and we only work with founders committed enough to launch. What we guarantee is the thing you actually want: the outcome."

**The Live-in-30 Guarantee:**
**"If your platform is not live in production, on infrastructure you own, passing every item on the Acceptance Checklist we co-signed at Blueprint approval, within 30 days of kickoff, we keep the full named team on your build for free until it passes, your 60-day Founders' Shield extends to 180 days at zero cost, and on the split plan your final $50,000 stays in your account until the day it passes."**

Your cash is never at risk of vanishing: the work sits in your accounts from day one, and the team works free until done. Given the choice between a refund and a launched platform, every founder takes the platform; this guarantee is engineered so 100% could qualify and none want to.

**Conditions (the exact client actions that produce success, nothing else):**
1. Blueprint and Acceptance Checklist signed before kickoff.
2. Requested assets, credentials, and third-party access delivered within 48 hours of each request (the concierge does the heavy lifting, you approve).
3. One named decision-maker (or deputy); feedback at the three scheduled decision gates within 1 business day.
4. No net-new scope beyond the two Swap Tokens.

Miss a condition and the clock pauses, it never voids; it resumes when you do. Delays caused by third-party approvals outside anyone's control (app store reviews, API access grants) pause the clock for both sides, documented in the decision log.

## 6. Scarcity & Urgency Mechanics

**Scarcity (honest total business cap):** Nikolytics runs a maximum of **three concurrent builds**, period. Not positioning, the real capacity of a senior-only bench, and exactly why the 30 days holds. Fill level is published on the site and stated on every call ("2 of 3 build slots are filled"). When full, the waitlist opens: numbered positions, Blueprint purchasers hold priority, and when a slot opens the top of the list gets 72 hours of first right of refusal before it moves down. Slots are never oversold. Sell-outs are announced publicly, and any future price raise is announced, never silent: "the price goes up next quarter, get in now."

**Urgency (one ethical method: cohort-based rolling):** builds kick off on the **first and third Monday of every month**, because kickoff choreography (accounts, sandboxes, extraction sessions) is batched. Script: "If we sign by Thursday, your Blueprint Sprint starts Monday and you are live in production 30 days after kickoff. Miss it, and the next kickoff is two weeks out, if the slot still exists." If a prospect misses a cohort, sell the wait: more prep time and a scheduled slot, never a discount. The single kickoff-locked bonus (Section 7) is bonus-level urgency under rule 11, subordinate to the cohort method, not a second mechanism.
