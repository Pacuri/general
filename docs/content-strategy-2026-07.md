# Nikolytics Content Strategy

**Version:** 1.0 draft, 2026-07-09. Output of the content identity session (decision log 2026-07-04, item 16). Pending Nikola's review. Companion decision log: `decision-log-2026-07-09.md`. This document supersedes Section 4 (Content Engine) of channel-plan v1, which was never approved.

---

## 1. The one-line identity

Nikola is the investigator of the unfiltered money story of startups: what it actually feels like to raise and to live with the money, told through 100 conversations with freshly funded, non-technical founders, with Nikola answering the hardest question first.

The spine question of the entire operation, asked of every guest and answered by Nikola in episode zero: **"Are you anxious?"**

## 2. What content is for

Do they like you, and do they believe you. Content builds trust, familiarity, and audience. It does not sell. The company appears as the credential that funds the work, never as a pitch. Give in public, ask in private. The first client comes from outreach and referrals; content is the moat, the "he's real" layer behind every DM, and the long fuse on a compounding audience.

## 3. The audience and the guests

- **Audience:** broad startup world. Aspiring founders, operators, the startup-curious. Reach is a goal.
- **Guests:** freshly funded US founders (pre-seed to Series B) with **no technical co-founder and no founding engineers**. Guests double as prospects; the guest list IS the avatar.
- **Targeting bubble (Nikola's decision, consultant objection on record):** content features and targets non-technical founders only. Highly technical people are not targeted or featured. AI-assisted building is acknowledged openly when asked; nothing is hidden, but the lens stays on the non-technical founder's experience. Review trigger: at conversation 25, or when the "why not just AI it" objection recurs in comments, Nikola privately reviews the demand-study data and reconfirms or adjusts.

## 4. The Show (engine 1: believe)

An investigation, not a guru podcast. Public counter on every piece: conversation X of 100.

**Format:** war-story interviews, 45 minutes, remote, recorded. Plus a **verified-anonymous confession track** for stories founders cannot tell on record (down rounds, bridges, shutdowns): identity hidden, numbers verified by Nikola before publishing, and that verification stated in every episode.

**The rules, stated publicly and never broken:**
1. No pitch, on the call or in the edit. If a guest asks what Nikolytics does, plain answer, separate call.
2. Guest veto: they see the cut first and can kill anything.
3. Guests keep the clips and use them freely.
4. The four demand-study fields are logged privately on every conversation (trigger, objections, last-vendor story, source), including whatever guests say about AI tools. Sealed feed outside, honest data inside.

**Episode zero:** Nikola on camera answering "Are you anxious?" about his own company before asking anyone else. Content anxiety admitted plainly; money anxiety stated truthfully without private numbers. The interviewer earns the question by going first.

**Interview question bank:** the 44 questions from the funding research report, strongest-demand first, plus the standing segment for every guest: "Did you try building it with AI tools first? What happened?"

**Name:** to be picked from three test candidates: "Are You Anxious?", "After the Wire", "Before the Flex". Consultant recommendation on record: Are You Anxious?. Test via page title and signature link across outreach batches.

## 5. The Daily (engine 2: like)

Squad content: the office, the brainstorms, Nikola processing what founder X told him, the philosophical register with real fuel. Nikola is the main character; Mario, Aleksije, and the team appear as cast.

**Drama doctrine (decision):** engineered real stakes with dramatic editing and storytelling. Events are always real: real deadlines, real countdowns (the Live-in-30 guarantee is a built-in reality format), real decisions. The edit may be cinematic (pacing, music, cliffhangers). Fabricated events, invented numbers, and staged outcomes are banned. The audience polices authenticity; the honesty religion applies to content.

**Boundary rule:** honesty about the craft, the past, and the journey, always. Desperation signals about the current pipeline, never. Published fill levels are mechanics; "we need clients" vibes are poison to a premium offer. The investigator is curious, not hungry.

## 6. The funnel map

| Layer | Asset | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Top | Short-form clips (Daily + Show cuts) | Reach and liking; the bubble's front door |
| Middle | Full interviews, confession episodes, synthesis long-form | Believing |
| Bottom | Website, Build Diary, LinkedIn profile, Teardown | Converting the warm; where the business is visible |

Content asks feed the show, not the pipeline: the standing ask is "know a founder who just raised? Introduce us." Give:ask stays high; sales asks live in outreach and on the site only.

## 7. Platforms and cadence

- **Short-form:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts. One clip, three uploads.
- **Long-form:** the Show on YouTube. Podcast guesting as secondary long-form (pitching folded into the outreach motion).
- **LinkedIn:** outreach surface and profile only. Not a content platform. (Supersedes channel-plan v1.)
- **Cadence, phase 1 (pre-office, pre-intern):** 3 to 4 clips per week, phone camera, batch-filmed. Interviews as booked.
- **Cadence, phase 2 (office + intern live):** daily clip, weekly interview, office footage. Sized properly at the office move (about a month out), when the founder time budget is re-set. Until then the logged 3 hours per day stands.
- INTIIMI is never referenced in any content. The NDA engagement is never referenced. Paragons content is cleared (Shane's permission in place, verbal); every co-post carries the standing disclosure line: "I hold a small equity stake in Shane's umbrella company."
- Heavy-story tone rule: when a founder's story involves death, illness, or grief, playfulness is stripped from every artifact touching it, and Nikola rereads before anything ships.

## 8. The sourcing and booking machine (operational)

1. **Feed:** daily prompt run pulls 10 fresh non-technical funded founders (prompt v3 plus patches: no founding engineers, build-need signal required, DTC/CPG skip, agency-built product flagged as top-prospect signal, reaction_beat column).
2. **Ledger:** `docs/leads.csv` is the dedupe memory and status tracker. Every run checks it; every lead carries a status. Paste current names into any external run, or schedule the pull where the ledger lives.
3. **Template:** Nikola's control version with reaction beat, locked for the first 100 sends. Subject pattern: "the [detail] story". No congrats, no raise amounts, no pitch.
4. **Sequence:** email, then day-3 LinkedIn connect one-liner, then day 8-10 bump, recycle at 3 months. One genuine comment on their announcement post between touches.
5. **Waves:** practice interviews first (Aleksije, Shane orbit), then wave 1 sends, crown jewels (agency-built products, e.g. CompanAIn, Chptr) only after two practice interviews are on tape.
6. **Compounding:** every interview ends with "who do you know with a raise story nobody's heard?"
7. Expectation, labeled as planning assumption: 10 asks/day books roughly 2 interviews/week warm; 100 conversations is a 9 to 12 month season, and the public counter makes duration the format.

## 11. First two weeks of content (concrete, phase 1, phone camera)

Bank items 1 through 4 before publishing anything, then release on rhythm.

1. **Launch clip:** "I'm going to interview 100 freshly funded founders about what it actually feels like. Counter starts at zero." Premise, the gap ("everyone sees the announcement, nobody hears what happened before it"), the rules.
2. **Episode zero clip:** "The question I'll ask all 100: are you anxious? Fair is fair, I go first." The content-anxiety confession, verbatim from the heart.
3. **The rules clip:** no pitch ever, guest veto, verified-anonymous track explained. Trust-building, differentiating.
4. **Booking diary clip:** "I emailed 12 founders this week. Here's what happened." Replies, silences, the counter moving or not. The machine itself is content.
5. **Practice interview 1 (Aleksije):** two cuts: one story clip, one Nikola-synthesis clip ("what a guy who runs several businesses told me about stress").
6. **Practice interview 2 (Shane orbit):** same two-cut treatment.
7. **Synthesis clip:** "Three things I learned from my first three conversations." The philosophical register, fed by real material.
8. **First real founder interview:** three cuts: their wildest moment, the anxiety answer, Nikola's reaction.
9. **Reaction clip:** first outreach replies ("a founder I've never met said yes in nine minutes"). Only if true.
10. **Counter update + the one ask:** "X of 100. Know a founder who just raised? Introduce us." The only ask of the fortnight.

## 12. Metrics

Monthly: followers and total reach per platform, growth rate absolute and percent. Weekly: outreach sent, replies, interviews booked, interviews recorded, counter position. Zero performance claims in public content until real numbers exist. No fabricated or implied stats anywhere, ever.
