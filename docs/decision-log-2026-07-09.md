# Nikolytics Decision Log, Content Strategy Session of 2026-07-09

Extends the decision logs of 2026-07-03 (`business-context` in the hormozi skill) and 2026-07-04. This session is the dedicated content identity session queued as item 16 in the 2026-07-04 log. Full design detail lives in `content-strategy-2026-07.md`; this log records the decisions.

Style rules in force: plain and direct, no em dashes anywhere, facts labeled.

## Decisions

1. **Content platforms.** Short-form video is primary: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, one clip uploaded to all three. Long-form lives on YouTube plus podcast guesting. LinkedIn is demoted to an outreach surface and profile only; it is not a content platform. Channel-plan v1 Section 4 (LinkedIn-only content engine) is superseded; it was never approved. (Nikola)

2. **Content identity: the investigator.** The core content asset is an investigation: 100 interviews with freshly funded US founders about the unfiltered money story, before and after the wire, with a public counter. Spine question asked of every guest: "Are you anxious?" Nikola answers it first on camera (episode zero) before asking anyone else. (Nikola, developed in session)

3. **Guest and target filter: non-technical founders only.** Guests and content targets are funded founders with no technical co-founder and no founding engineers. Guests double as prospects; the guest list is the avatar. Highly technical people are not targeted or featured. AI-assisted building is acknowledged openly when asked; nothing is concealed. Consultant objection to the targeting bubble recorded (risk: the AI-easy narrative lives inside the non-technical guest pool; risk of corrupting the demand study if filtering reaches the private data). Mitigations adopted: the four demand-study fields are logged privately and unfiltered on every conversation; review checkpoint at conversation 25 or on recurring "why not just AI it" comment objections. (Nikola; objection and mitigations logged)

4. **Show rules.** No pitch on calls or in edits, ever; the series runs on give-until-they-ask. Guest veto on the cut. Guests keep and freely use the clips. Verified-anonymous confession track for off-record stories: identity hidden, numbers verified by Nikola before publishing, verification stated publicly. (Nikola)

5. **Drama doctrine.** Engineered real stakes with dramatic editing and storytelling. Events, numbers, deadlines, and outcomes are always real; the edit may be cinematic. Fabricated events and staged outcomes are banned. (Nikola)

6. **Squad and roles.** Mario and Aleksije join as partners and shareholders, split to be defined (open item). Mario owns talent acquisition, training, and capacity, and works as an engineer in the early phase. Aleksije manages employees, scouts talent to Mario, and enforces performance. Content operations (capture, edit, post) go to a film-student intern recruited on an internship-now-pay-later basis; the pay-later promise goes in writing (open item). (Nikola)

7. **Engineers-on-camera amendment.** The operating manual rule "the two engineers are never pulled into marketing work" is amended: the protection covers delivery time, not camera presence. Team members may appear in content voluntarily; nobody performs for content at the cost of delivery hours. (Nikola)

8. **Serbia.** Not thematized in the content identity. Not hidden, not mentioned as a theme. (Nikola)

9. **Founder time budget.** The logged 3 hours per day for outreach plus content stands until the office move (about a month out), at which point the budget and cadence are re-sized. Content quotas in phase 1 are sized to roughly 30 founder-minutes per day via weekly batch filming. (Nikola)

10. **Shane content permission.** The formal co-content barter agreement (2026-07-04 log, open item 11) is replaced by a lighter gate: one written confirmation message from Shane covering filming and publishing the two app builds, the equity disclosure line in every co-post, and a 48-hour veto window. Verbal agreement already exists (Nikola statement); the written message is a pending open item because the publish gate requires written form. USOPC phrasing rules and the athlete-figure verification gate remain unchanged. (Nikola, modified in session)

11. **Sourcing machine adopted.** Daily prompt-driven pull of 10 freshly funded non-technical founders; dedupe ledger at `docs/leads.csv`; outreach template locked as Nikola's control version (with reaction beat) for the first 100 sends; follow-up cadence email, day-3 LinkedIn touch, day 8-10 bump, 3-month recycle; crown-jewel leads (agency-built products) held until two practice interviews are on tape. Agency-built product added as a top-prospect signal. (Nikola + session)

12. **Series name.** Three candidates go to test: "Are You Anxious?", "After the Wire", "Before the Flex". Consultant recommendation: "Are You Anxious?". Decision by Nikola or by the test data. (Open)

13. **Checklist over calendar.** Workstreams are sequenced as an ordered checklist with dependency order, not calendar dates. (Nikola)

14. **Foundations note.** The short-form audience build is the trigger-builder for the parked foundations segment (2026-07-04 log, decision 12). No action; noted for the revisit trigger. (Session note)
