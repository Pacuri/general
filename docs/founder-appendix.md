## 4. CAC governors (derivations)

The $100M growth bar: one client's 30-day profit funds getting and servicing two more (cost per next client = CAC + $35,000 fulfillment).

- Naked build: 215,000 − CAC ≥ 2 × (CAC + 35,000) → CAC ≤ **~$48,000**.
- With the Traction Block attached (adds $80,000 revenue, $21,000 reserve): CAC ≤ **~$68,000**.
- Blueprint funnel (at the ≥60% conversion target): Blueprint CAC ≤ ~$28,800 hard ceiling; $15,000-$20,000 working target.
- The 3:1 LTGP:CAC floor sits near $71,000 (naked) / $93,000 (blended) and is therefore not the binding constraint; **the governor is.** Paid spend paces to the governor, never to the floor.
- Mark's $25,000 fee: 8.6:1 against naked-build LTGP; comfortably inside the governor. His legacy ~20% (which would be $50,000 on a build) passes the 3:1 floor but breaks the $48k governor; that is the principled basis for the agreed flat structure. Existing 20% arrangement details stay with Nikola and are superseded for Nikolytics builds by the written terms.
